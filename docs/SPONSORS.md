## Judge-visible demonstration

Historical verification delivery audit (2026-09-19): NVIDIA credentials were absent at that checkpoint; the later setup task preserved an existing local NVIDIA key without validating it. The subsequent [Brev retest](handoffs/dev-a/brev-live-retest.md), merged in PR15, used the user's existing H100 and the same five consented synthetic native-text clauses twice. Exact fields passed 3/5 and then 5/5 without code/prompt/validator changes; citation/literal and unreviewed-execution gates passed all ten attempts. Preserve the failed first run: this tiny repeated corpus does not establish general model accuracy. No OCR, hosted evidence check or live upload/worker/human-review flow was tested. Optional Gemini and other provider live checks remain unclaimed. No new resources or domains were provisioned by the retest or this integration task. SQLite is tested locally and PostgreSQL in CI; neither demonstrates a live Tiger Data service or a performance advantage.

`python scripts/eval_nemotron.py` runs a five-clause synthetic semantic gate evaluation with 3 exact fixture outputs and 2 deliberately wrong candidates (relative-date invention and a one-cent error). All 5 remain withheld from execution pending review; both faulty candidates are rejected. These counts are local gate behavior, not model accuracy. `--live --consent-external` explicitly opts into the configured text provider (hosted NVIDIA or private Brev), reports failures, and never falls back to fixtures. It does not exercise OCR or establish full upload/worker/human-review accuracy. The model never verifies plans or computes money.

Open the synthetic workspace and its six sources, then follow docs/DEMO.md. The data-mode banner is separate from Settings → Connected services. The settings panel exposes unavailable/local/configured states and provider errors; configuration alone is not proof of a live call. Show the exact integer-cent acceptance arithmetic and source-driven approval change without implying that offline fixtures were extracted by a sponsor model.

With real keys supplied server-side, `python scripts/smoke_providers.py` creates and deletes a synthetic session and makes minimal requests for extraction, the explicitly selected evidence model, and configured audio. Calls consume quota and may consume credits. The latest missing-key smoke reports NVIDIA extraction/evidence and ElevenLabs unavailable; the configured but unselected Gemini key was not used. A successful smoke response is only a credential/connectivity check; a full extraction/verification or audio workflow needs its own test. Unselected Gemini credentials never trigger a request.

For a live demonstration, upload only synthetic evidence, explicitly authorize external processing, run the worker, inspect job progress, review every consequential rule, then calculate. Model agreement does not prove correctness, and provider outputs never approve themselves. Original pages, exact quotes/offsets, financial amounts/dates, condition resolution, review and third-party approval remain inspectable.
